# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Critique prompts, rules, and code — not the person who wrote them.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good intent and ask before assuming a mistake.
- Give feedback that names the behavior and the impact.
- Accept that "this rule fired on my prompt and I disagree" is a useful bug report.
- Respect privacy. Do not paste other people's prompts, credentials, or customer data into issues.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination based on identity, background, or experience level.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberate bad-faith argument.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through the repository's [security advisory form](https://github.com/noxaplus-star/noxaplus-promptlens/security/advisories/new) or by contacting NoxaPlus directly. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may warn, remove content, or block participation depending on severity.
